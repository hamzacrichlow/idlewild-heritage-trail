LeeJon’s Novelty Shop

Listen to the Story
Narrated by Chris J. Grier, Historian & Yates Township Supervisor
0:00 0:00

Built in 1949 by John and Leona Simmons, LeeJon’s began as a confectionery and gift shop offering a soda bar, souvenirs and everyday goods. Places like LeeJon’s show the range of Black-owned businesses that once supported Idlewild’s thriving visitor economy — and the possibilities for reusing historic commercial spaces today.
