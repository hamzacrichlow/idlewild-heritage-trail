The Flamingo

Listen to the Story
Narrated by Chris J. Grier, Historian & Yates Township Supervisor
0:00 0:00

Opened by Detroit hotelier Phil Giles in the 1950s, the Flamingo became one of Idlewild’s signature nightspots on Williams Island. Music, dancing and live entertainment drew visitors from across the Midwest and helped earn Idlewild its reputation as the “Summer Apollo of Michigan.” The club stood at the center of an entertainment culture created for Black audiences during segregation.
